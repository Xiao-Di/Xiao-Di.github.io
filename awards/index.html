Second Prize, 2026 Xiong'an Aerospace Information Innovation Technology Competition

2026.07

Organizing Committee of the Xiong'an Future City Scenario Expo (organized by the Hebei Provincial Development and Reform Commission)

Integrated Satellite Internet and Intelligent Computing Technology — In response to the new industry landscape and emerging development challenges, this project follows the trend toward in-orbit intelligence for satellite Internet. To support the iterative development and validation of satellite, network, and application intelligence, it establishes a ground-based parallel verification environment and an open validation platform, and develops standardized, reusable satellite intelligent-computing payloads. The project builds full-chain capabilities spanning ground training, model deployment to space, space-based inference, in-orbit updates, and satellite–ground collaboration. It enables unified management and coordinated scheduling of core AI resources such as satellite–ground computing power, models, and data, supports the transition from connectivity to intelligent services, and provides core support for the integrated innovation of Xiong'an's digital economy and aerospace information industry.
