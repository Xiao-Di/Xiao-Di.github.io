Symbol-wise Methods to Improve Sensing Performance of Single Carrier Waveform

Yuhan Long, Yangyang Niu, Yunxin Geng, Shang Liu, Zhiqing Wei

IEEE Wireless Communications and Networking Conference Workshops (WCNCW), 2026

This paper investigates the inherent trade-off between power-amplifier efficiency measured by temporal power variance and sensing performance represented by expected integrated ranging sidelobe for uplink ISAC orthogonal communication bases, and proposes both convex symbol-wise optimization schemes and a low-complexity iterative clip-filter algorithm to enhance the 2D periodic autocorrelation-function sidelobe performance of CP-SC waveforms under constrained communication EVM loss.

Beyond Single-Band: Analysis and Resource Allocation for Multi-band ISAC Systems

Haotian Liu, Zhiqing Wei, Xingwang Li, Yunxin Geng, Qixun Zhang, Zhiyong Feng

arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.08068, 2026

This paper derives closed-form detection and false-alarm probabilities for multi-band ISAC OFDM signals using the generalized likelihood ratio test and proposes an ADMM-driven joint power and time-frequency resource allocation scheme to maximize target-detection performance, with simulations verifying theoretical accuracy and demonstrating prominent detection gains and enhanced robustness over single-band counterparts.

DMSNet: Cross-Band Learning for Multi-Target Sensing in Multi-Band ISAC

Haotian Liu, Zhiqing Wei, Quanjiang Zhao, Lin Wang, Yunxin Geng, Xingwang Li, Zhiyong Feng

arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.17655, 2026

This paper proposes DMSNet, a coarse-to-fine dual-band multi-target sensing neural network that adaptively fuses cross-band complementary features to jointly estimate target count as well as range, velocity and angle, achieving substantial accuracy improvements and reduced inference runtime compared with existing multi-band ISAC sensing baselines.
