About

I am an engineer with Shanghai Satellite Network Research Institute Co. Ltd. (State Key Laboratory of Satellite Network), and also a D.Eng student at the School of Information and Communication Engeering, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, advised by Prof. Zhiqing Wei.

Prior to this, I obtained a B.Eng and a M.Eng degree (advised by Prof. Kan Zheng and Assoc. Prof. Long Zhao) with National Scholarship from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications in 2020 and 2023, respectivbely.

My current research focuses on space-ground integrated networks, space-based computing, constellation management and deep learning.
